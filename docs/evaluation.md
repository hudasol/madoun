# Model evaluation

Generated by `npm run eval` (16 synthetic weeks, seeds 100 to 115; the first half fits the learned challenger, the second half scores every method). Deterministic: rerunning gives the same numbers. The demo itself uses seed 2026, which is never part of this evaluation.

> **Read this first.** The synthetic generator decides which shipments hide a violation from the trader's compliance score and from under-declared value. The rule engine reads the same facts. Scores here are therefore optimistic and partly circular. This evaluation can expose a weak, uneven or beaten rule set; it cannot show the rules work on real traffic. Real validation needs labelled historical outcomes from customs.

## Population
- 3840 shipments, 510 with a hidden violation (13.3%, 95% range 12.2% to 14.4%).
- Scored on a held-out half: 1920 shipments, 239 violations.

## Do the lanes order risk?
| Lane | Shipments | With a violation | Rate | 95% range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| green | 2960 | 179 | 6.0% | 5.2% to 7.0% |
| amber | 743 | 251 | 33.8% | 30.5% to 37.3% |
| red | 137 | 80 | 58.4% | 50.0% to 66.3% |

Madoun checks 22.9% of shipments (amber and red) and catches 64.9% of violations (95% range 60.8% to 68.8%). Precision of a check is 37.6%. **179 violations (35.1%) passed through the green lane.** Random audits of green traffic exist to measure exactly this.

## Rules against simpler and learned alternatives (held-out half)
| Method | AUC (95% bootstrap range) | Average precision | Violations found at the same effort |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Madoun rules (hand-set weights) | 0.80 (0.76 to 0.83) | 0.36 | 67.8% |
| Learned challenger (logistic regression, advisory) | 0.85 (0.82 to 0.88) | 0.44 | 75.7% |
| Trader compliance score alone | 0.78 (0.74 to 0.82) | 0.34 | 63.6% |
| Declared value alone | 0.65 (0.62 to 0.68) | 0.17 | 43.5% |
| Random | 0.50 (0.46 to 0.54) | 0.14 | 22.2% |

"Same effort": each method may check as many shipments as the rules do (the number sent to amber or red) and we count the violations among them.

What this says:
- The rules rank risk far better than chance (AUC 0.80 against 0.50).
- The trader's compliance score alone reaches AUC 0.78, close to the full rule set (difference 0.02). Much of the engine's strength comes from that one fact.
- The learned challenger scores 0.85 (+0.05) and finds 75.7% of violations at the rules' effort against 67.8%. The ranges overlap, so the gain is not established. It is not used to decide lanes: promotion needs a stable gain on held-out data, calibration, no worse burden on any group, and sign-off.

### Challenger coefficients (standardised; positive raises risk)
| Feature | Weight |
|---|---:|
| declared value looks low | 0.473 |
| compliance score (lower is riskier) | -0.366 |
| confirmed past findings | 0.287 |
| authorised operator | -0.159 |
| customs category points | -0.138 |
| first shipment from this origin | -0.051 |
| documents missing or invalid | 0.029 |
| road consignment | 0.015 |
| declared value looks high | 0.000 |

The generator does not use goods category, route or document gaps to decide violations, so the challenger rightly gives them about zero or odd-signed weight. That is a property of the synthetic data, not a finding about customs.

## Who bears the checks?
| Group | Shipments | Checked | Checks that found nothing | 95% range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Trader compliance below 60 | 516 | 100.0% | 49.0% | 44.7% to 53.3% |
| Trader compliance 60 to 79 | 478 | 25.3% | 80.2% | 72.2% to 86.3% |
| Trader compliance 80 and above | 2846 | 8.5% | 81.9% | 76.6% to 86.2% |
| Authorised operators | 1076 | 1.6% | 88.2% | 65.7% to 96.7% |
| Other traders | 2764 | 31.2% | 61.9% | 58.6% to 65.1% |

Every shipment from a low-compliance trader is sent to amber or red. That is blanket treatment of a class of traders, not a per-shipment risk judgement, and it should be a stated policy choice (proportionality, TFA Article 7.4) rather than a side effect of weights.

## What the unbiased sample adds
The learning loop only sees outcomes for shipments something chose to check, so its hit rates describe a selected group. Madoun now audits flagged green shipments after release (never delaying them), and a hash-selected 1 in 20 of green traffic is a true random sample. Its outcome rate is the only base rate that is not shaped by the rules.
