# Model card: Madoun risk lanes

## What it is
A transparent, rule-based scorer. Each authority adds points for facts about the shipment (goods category, declared value against a reference, trader compliance score, past findings, first-time origin, road mode, missing or expiring documents) and subtracts points for authorised-operator status. The highest authority score sets the lane (green, amber or red). Every point has a reason a person can read. Weights are set by hand and are illustrative.

A learned **challenger** (logistic regression on the same facts) is trained and scored only inside the model check. It does not decide lanes.

## Intended use
- Decide how much attention a shipment gets, in parallel across authorities, before arrival.
- Always with an officer able to override, and with a hash-selected random audit of green traffic as a control.
- Never as the only basis for a penalty, and only on lawful factors (WCO/TFA Article 7.4 risk management: nothing about nationality, religion or similar).

## Out of scope
Real declarations, individual-level profiling, any automated adverse decision, any use of private persons' data.

## Evidence (synthetic only)
See [evaluation.md](evaluation.md), regenerated by `npm run eval`. Headline, 16 synthetic weeks:
- Lanes do order risk: green about 6%, amber about 34%, red about 58% of shipments with a hidden violation.
- The rules catch about 65% of violations by checking about 23% of shipments. About 35% pass through green.
- The rules rank better than chance (AUC 0.80) but only slightly better than the trader's compliance score alone (0.78).
- A learned challenger scores higher (0.85) but the ranges overlap, and it is overconfident at the top end.

**These numbers are optimistic.** The synthetic generator decides violations from the same facts the rules read. They prove the machinery works and can expose problems; they say nothing about real accuracy.

## Known weaknesses
1. **Selection bias in learning.** Outcomes exist only for shipments something chose to check. Madoun now audits flagged green shipments after release and treats the hash-selected 1 in 20 as a random sample. With small samples the base rate has a wide range, shown in the interface. Audits can themselves miss things (modelled detection 60%), so true rates are at least the observed ones.
2. **Blanket treatment.** Every shipment from a low-compliance trader is sent to amber or red, and about half of those checks find nothing. This is policy, not risk estimation, and should be decided openly.
3. **Dominance of one fact.** The trader's compliance score alone nearly matches the whole rule set on this data.
4. **Hand weights, no uncertainty.** Scores are not probabilities. Lane observed-rates are reported instead.
5. **Co-occurring signals.** Signals often appear together, so a per-signal hit rate cannot say which one mattered. Suggestions are prompts for a person, never automatic.

## Learning guardrails
- Suggestions only; a named officer approves; the multiplier is bounded to (0, 5] by validation; every approval is an event in the audit trail.
- Each suggestion states whether its 95% interval clears the decision threshold ("strong") or still crosses it ("thin").
- A suggestion's effect can be previewed by replaying the same synthetic week before approving.

## Promotion path for a challenger (champion/challenger)
1. Run in shadow on real, labelled history: no effect on lanes.
2. Require a gain in ranking and in violations found at equal effort whose ranges do not overlap, on data it was not fitted to.
3. Require calibrated probabilities (reliability within tolerance, Brier below the baseline).
4. Require no worse checking burden for any protected or sensitive group, and a documented answer for any group that bears more.
5. A person with authority signs off, and the previous model stays available for instant rollback.

## Monitoring plan (not yet built)
Track lane mix and violation rate per lane monthly; population-stability checks on input facts; audit outcomes against predicted rates; alert when green-lane audit hits rise. Retrain only through the promotion path.
