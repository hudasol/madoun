# Research notes

Why Madoun is a layer and not another portal.

- **WTO TFA Art. 7**: pre-arrival processing (7.1), release separate from final determination (7.3), risk management on objective criteria (7.4), post-clearance audit (7.5), release-time measurement (7.6), authorised operators (7.7). Art. 8: border agency cooperation.
- **WCO**: Data Model vocabulary for a shared data set; Time Release Study methodology (release time, lane, inaction time) used for the KPI definitions.
- **Single windows** usually solve *filing once*, but evidence checked by one agency is still re-checked by the next, and reviews often run in sequence. Madoun targets that gap: evidence receipts with explicit reuse rules, and a parallel review graph.
- **Risk engines** are often opaque. Madoun shows each signal and its reason, uses only permitted factors, and measures false interventions.
- **TradeLens (shut down 2022)**: lessons taken as design rules: federated custody of evidence, value for every participant, neutral governance, no data lock-in.
- **Known limits**: authority rules, response times and goods-to-approval mappings here come from secondary public sources and are illustrative. Real figures need validation with Abu Dhabi Customs and each authority.
